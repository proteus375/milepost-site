# Milepost Publisher Terms

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** DRAFT — not reviewed by counsel. Do not publish or rely on this document.

These terms apply when you share a course pack through Milepost. You accept them before your first
publication, and again whenever they change. We record which version you accepted and when.

If you publish on behalf of an organisation — a co-op — **both you and the organisation accept these
terms**, and both make the promises in section 2. An organisation accepts through one of its owners;
a member with publishing rights cannot accept on its behalf.

## 1. What you are granting

When you publish a pack you grant:

**To Pointer Technologies LLC**, a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to host, store,
reproduce, display and distribute your pack to Milepost subscribers, and to make the technical copies
and format conversions needed to do that. This licence lasts as long as the pack is listed, and
survives afterwards only to the extent needed for backups and for records we are required to keep.

**To subscribers who download it**, the rights set out in the Milepost Content Licence 1.0 — including
the right to adapt your pack and to publish their adaptation. **If you are not willing for your work
to be adapted and re-shared, do not publish it.**

You keep ownership of everything you publish. This is a licence, not a transfer.

## 2. What you are promising

You promise that:

1. **You have the right to share it.** Either you created it, or you have permission from whoever did,
   or it is genuinely free of copyright.
2. **It does not infringe anyone's rights** — copyright, trademark, privacy or anything else.
3. **It contains no personal information about any child** other than your own, and no personal
   information about your own children that you did not intend to make public. Names, photographs and
   submitted work belonging to children do not belong in a published pack.
4. **It complies with the Acceptable Use Policy.**

> ### Read this part
>
> Homeschool material is very often built around published curricula, workbooks, textbook pages and
> images found online. **Those are usually not yours to share**, even when you have paid for them, and
> even when you have substantially rearranged them.
>
> A worksheet you scanned from a purchased workbook, a chapter you photographed, a picture you found
> through an image search — none of these can be published here. What you can publish is what you
> wrote: your own lesson plans, your own questions, your own sequencing, your own explanations, and
> references that point to material rather than reproducing it.
>
> If you are unsure whether something is yours to share, it probably is not.

## 3. Indemnity

If someone brings a claim against Pointer Technologies LLC because of something you published, you
agree to cover the resulting costs, including reasonable legal fees.

For an organisation listing, you and the organisation are each responsible.

## 4. What we may do

1. **Review before publication.** Listings are moderated before they go live. We may decline a listing
   without giving a reason.
2. **Remove a listing at any time**, including after publication, and including on receipt of a
   copyright notice under the Copyright and Takedown Policy.
3. **Review adaptations of a removed pack.** When we remove a pack, we review the listings directly
   adapted from it and decide, for each, whether it carries the same problem. We do not automatically
   remove an adaptation; we do not automatically leave it.
4. **Suspend or end publishing rights** for repeated infringement, in line with the repeat infringer
   provisions of the Copyright and Takedown Policy.

## 5. Nothing is sold

Milepost does not sell course packs, does not pay publishers, and does not take a commission. Every
pack is shared, not traded. Subscribers pay Pointer Technologies LLC for access to the platform and
its shared content; no part of that reaches publishers.

You are not entitled to payment, revenue share, or any other compensation for what you publish, now or
if this changes in future.

## 6. Withdrawing a pack

You may unlist a pack at any time. Unlisting stops new downloads. It does not reach back into the
instances of subscribers who already downloaded it — their licence under the Content Licence is
permanent, and section 4 of that licence explains the one case where it is not.

If you unlist a pack, adaptations of it stay published. They were separately licensed when they were
made.

## 7. Changes

We may change these terms. Packs you have already published stay under the version you accepted;
publishing again requires accepting the current version.
