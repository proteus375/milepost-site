## 1. Who can use Milepost

You must be 18 or older to hold an account. **Children do not have accounts on Milepost.** A parent or
guardian holds the account and manages their children's records on their behalf. This is a
deliberate design decision, not an omission — see the Privacy Policy.

## 2. Your instance

Each household or co-op gets its own separate instance of the software, with its own database. Your
records are not stored alongside another family's.

## 3. Subscriptions

**What the subscription buys is access to other people's content** — the marketplace catalogue, and,
in a co-op, courses that other families in your co-op authored.

**It does not buy the use of the software.** Running your own homeschool — recording attendance,
setting work, grading it, keeping portfolios, producing transcripts, teaching from material you wrote
yourself — is not what you are paying for, and does not stop if you stop paying.

Billing is per household, monthly or annually, through Stripe. [[TO CONFIRM: price, trial terms,
refund policy]]

### If your subscription lapses

There is a grace period of [[TO CONFIRM: 14–30 days]] before anything changes.

**These stay available, paid or not, without exception:**

- Every child's profile, attendance, submitted work, grades and certificates
- Your portfolios and transcripts, on screen and printed
- A complete export of everything about your own children
- Anything you authored yourself
- Anything you downloaded from the marketplace while your subscription was current

**These stop:**

- Browsing, downloading from and publishing to the marketplace
- Reading course content that other families authored inside a shared co-op instance

We will not hold your children's records hostage over a payment. They may be needed for legal or
reporting purposes on a schedule that has nothing to do with a billing cycle, and they are yours.

### One exception, for co-op teachers

If you are teaching a co-op course that has already started, you keep access to that course's material
until the course's end date, whatever your billing status. The people harmed by cutting a class off
from its teacher are the students, not the teacher.

## 4. Co-ops

A co-op is a group of families sharing one instance. Joining one does not merge your records with
anyone else's — your children's records remain yours, visible to you, and exportable by you.

Each household in a co-op subscribes separately. **A co-op must tell prospective members that a
subscription is required before they sign up.** Enrolment in a co-op class is never blocked by a
billing status.

An organisation is a co-op's public identity on the marketplace. It persists independently of any one
person, so listings survive an organiser leaving or a co-op winding down. See the Publisher Terms.

## 5. Your content

## 6. Getting your data out

At any time, for any child, you can export a complete archive of their record — profile, attendance,
enrolments, submitted work and its files, grades, certificates, completion records, portfolio and
transcript. You do not need to be leaving, cancelling, or asking us. It is a button.

Some things are deliberately excluded from that archive because they are not the child's record:
private notes written by co-op staff, and conversations between other people.

Course packs are written by other families. We moderate listings, but we do not verify that they are

The service is provided as-is. [[TO CONFIRM: warranty disclaimer and limitation of liability — needs

## 9. Transcripts and attested records

Milepost produces transcripts that distinguish clearly between coursework certified by a co-op or
instructor and coursework attested by a parent. This distinction appears on the document itself, not
just in our database, and parent-attested credit is labelled and excluded from any weighted average.

We do this because a transcript may end up in front of an admissions officer, and a document that
blurred the two would mislead them. **You must not present a parent-attested record as a certified
one.**
