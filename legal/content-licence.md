This licence covers every course pack shared through Milepost. There is only one — a pack does not
carry different terms depending on who published it, and you never have to check whether two packs
can be combined.

**Milepost** means the service operated by Pointer Technologies LLC.
**A pack** means a course and its contents shared through Milepost by a subscriber or an organisation.
**The publisher** means whoever shared it.
**You** means the subscriber who downloaded it.

## 1. What you may do

While your household subscription is current you may download a pack. Once you have downloaded it,
you may, for as long as you like:

1. **Use it to teach the children in your own household**, or in a co-op you belong to.
2. **Adapt it** — rewrite it, cut it, add to it, translate it, combine it with other packs or with
   your own material. Adapting is expected, not merely tolerated.
3. **Share your adaptation back to Milepost**, subject to section 3.
4. **Keep your own records of the work your children did**, including anything they produced, without
   limit and regardless of anything else in this licence.

This licence is personal to your household, non-exclusive, and cannot be transferred to anyone else.

## 2. What you may not do

1. **You may not sell it**, or any adaptation of it, or charge anyone for access to it.
2. **You may not redistribute it outside Milepost** — not on another platform, not by email to people
   outside your household or co-op, not on a public website or file-sharing service.
3. **You may not remove or falsify attribution.**

Sharing a pack with the other families in a co-op you belong to, through Milepost, is not
redistribution. Posting it publicly is.

## 3. Sharing an adaptation

If you publish an adaptation, it is published under this same licence, and Milepost records which pack
it was adapted from.

Milepost shows the immediate source of an adaptation and nothing further back. If you adapt a pack
that was itself an adaptation, your listing names the pack you actually worked from — not a chain of
everyone before them. This is deliberate: on material that gets reused often, a full ancestry would be
unreadable and would not tell anyone anything they need.

**Publishing requires a current subscription.** Reading and adapting what you have already downloaded
does not.

## 4. How long this lasts

**Permanently, for anything you downloaded while your subscription was current.** If your subscription
lapses or you cancel, everything already in your instance stays yours to read, teach from and adapt.
You keep it.

What lapsing stops is reaching for more: browsing, downloading and publishing all require a current
subscription.

Two limits on that permanence:

1. **Content removed for infringement is the exception.** If a pack is taken down because the
   publisher did not have the right to share it, this licence ends for that pack — including copies
   already downloaded. Nobody can grant rights they never held, and we cannot licence to you what was
   not the publisher's to give. We will tell you if this affects something you downloaded.
2. **Courses shared inside a co-op instance are not covered by this licence at all.** Material another
   family authored inside a co-op you belong to is read where it sits; it was never downloaded and
   never licensed to you. Access to it follows your co-op membership and your subscription. See the
   Terms of Service.

## 5. Your own records are never affected

Nothing in this licence — including its ending under section 4 — touches your children's records.
Attendance, grades, submitted work, portfolios, transcripts and completion records are yours, remain
readable and printable, and can be exported in full at any time. If a pack is removed, the record that
your child did that work stays.

## 6. No warranty

Packs are shared by other families and co-ops. Milepost reviews listings before they go live, but does
not verify that a pack is accurate, complete, suitable for your child, or compliant with your state's
requirements. **You are responsible for what you teach and for meeting your own state's homeschooling
requirements.** Packs are provided as-is.

## 7. Version

This is version 1.0. A pack is licensed under the version in force when it was published; changing
this licence does not change the terms of packs already shared.
