# Milepost Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** DRAFT — not reviewed by counsel. Do not publish or rely on this document.

---

This policy applies to everything you put into Milepost, and especially to anything you publish to the
marketplace where other families will see it.

## Do not publish material that is not yours

The most common problem on a platform like this is not malice; it is scanned workbook pages. See the
Publisher Terms for what this means in practice. It is the single most likely way to get a listing
removed.

## Do not publish anything identifying a child

No names, photographs, video, audio, dates of birth, addresses or submitted work belonging to a
child — including your own. A published pack is a lesson plan, not a scrapbook.

This is the one rule we will enforce without discussion.

## Do not publish

- Sexual content, or anything sexualising a minor in any way
- Material that harasses, threatens or demeans people, or that attacks people on the basis of who they
  are
- Instructions for making weapons, explosives, or anything else designed to injure
- Material promoting self-harm or disordered eating
- Malware, or anything designed to interfere with the service or another user's instance
- Deliberate falsehoods about matters of health or safety presented as fact

## Teaching your own beliefs is not a violation

Milepost is used by families with a wide range of religious, political and pedagogical convictions,
and packs will reflect that. Material teaching a viewpoint — religious, secular, political,
traditional or otherwise — is welcome, and disagreeing with a pack's viewpoint is not grounds for
reporting it.

What is not welcome is content attacking people rather than advancing a position.

[[TO CONFIRM: this section is a product and community decision as much as a legal one. It sets the
tone of the marketplace and it should be your words, not ours]]

## Do not misuse the service

- Do not share your account, or use one subscription to serve households that should hold their own
- Do not attempt to reach another household's or co-op's instance
- Do not scrape, bulk-download or mirror the catalogue
- Do not misrepresent who you are, or publish as an organisation you do not belong to

## Reporting

Every listing has a report button. Copyright complaints go through the Copyright and Takedown Policy,
which has its own process and its own address.

## What happens

Depending on what happened, we may remove a listing, suspend publishing rights, or close an account.
Repeated copyright infringement leads to loss of publishing rights and account termination, as set out
in the Copyright and Takedown Policy.

Your children's records are not affected by an enforcement action against your publishing, and remain
exportable.
